The sounds of Beluma

Beluma has 18 consonants and 5 vowels. The letters are always pronounced the same way.

Consonants

LetterSound (IPA)Like in English
b d f g h j k l m n p r s t v w z/b d f g h j k l m n p r s t v w z/as written; j = *y*es, r rolled, w = *w*et, x = k+s

The acute accent (béla) marks the stressed vowel. The stress never moves when suffixes are added: béla + -s = bélas.

Word-building

Beluma is designed to be extended by its speakers. Combine roots head-first and the engine understands the new word automatically — even when it has never been written down before.

Roots are raw material

  • néla = water, sula = big → nela-sula = ocean
  • féma = eat, pira = tool → fema-pira = spoon
  • fira = bird, -nira = small → fira-nira = small bird
  • luma = light, saro = star → luma-saro = flashlight

Any new word is real

Type an unlisted compound — nela-fura-pira (water-fruit-tool = watering can) — and the translator analyses it root by root. Coin it, use it, and it belongs in the dictionary the same day. That is how real languages grow.

fura-nira-s s gavo.
Small fruits are good.

Grammar at a glance

Word order

Subject – Verb – Object, like English:

Mi leva tu.
I love you.

Adjectives come first: béla lomas = beautiful trees.

Possessors come first: mi-la nésa = my house.

Prepositions: na (in), po (to), dé (from), vél (with).

Say something

Sala! Havi tu s?
Hello! How are you?
Mi s réva. Mi né féma fura.
I'm happy. I ate fruit.
Lé fira wé hura na lé hula.
The bird will fly in the sky.
Kvo tu gai po nésa?
Are you going home?
du gai! Du lísa.
Don't go! Leave.
