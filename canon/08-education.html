Lía Beluma — Canon 08: Education & Examination

Rev 1 (Phase 5). Defines the official course and certification, and one hard rule:

the exam never runs ahead of the dictionary. Every word in any paper must exist in

h.txt at the VTXT the paper is printed against, so a learner can always check the answer

with the reference translator.

1. The course

  • 40 lessons, four books (A1→B1), housed in lessons/: - book-01 (1–10) A1 · book-02 (11–20) A2 · book-03 (21–30) B1 · book-04 (31–40) B1+ reading & composition.
  • Each lesson: objective → grammar → vocabulary → examples → drills with a key.
  • The 8 legacy quick-lessons in lessons.html remain the "20-minute" taster and overlap book-01; the books are canonical.

3. The examination

  • Six levels A1–C2 conforming to the Council scale; ≥60% pass, ≥50% per section.
  • Paper blueprint (six sections, weights 25/25/20/15/10/5): reading → grammar → translation → listening → writing → speaking.
  • Marks: translation 1 pt/item (half-point for grammar-only errors); register fidelity is assessed from B1 up; elegant canon-06-clean alternatives are accepted if the examiner records them.

4. The dictionary lock

  • Papers print VTXT 3.x on the front sheet; the examiner's key cites the same version.
  • Coining for teaching is allowed in the classroom but must clear the coiner (tools/coiner.js, canon 06) before any exam item uses it. A learner's coin that passes V1–V5 verdicts is never marked wrong for novelty.

5. Teacher & university

  • Teacher certificate: three units (methods, practice, assessment); requires ≥B2 in the exam. See teacher/README.md.
  • University: four-semester track (course grammar, corpus & translation, literature, lexicography with a defended coin). 60% written + 20% oral + 20% portfolio.

6. Revision policy

Education content follows the dictionary lock and may be revised (Review Rev) without

canon numbers changing; any change to the exam blueprint itself supersedes this section

and bumps this canon's revision.
