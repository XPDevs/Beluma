Status: Normative.

A clock or watch is vora-pira. These are ordinary count nouns (§5.2):

kvín vora five hours, tuo mída two minutes.

14.2 Telling the time

The hour is counted with vora; the minute follows with e (and):

  • Ona vora. — One o'clock. (01:00)
  • Tro vora e trósa. — 3:13. (three hours and thirteen)
  • Diza vora e vinta-ona. — 10:21.
  • Half past: tro vora e duon 3:30; quarter: tro vora e kvarparta 3:15.
  • ante to, pos past: tiza vora pos trósa? — preferred form is H e M only; use mísa trósa na tro vora thirteen to three for "to" times.
  • 24-hour clock is used in Technical and Formal ($17:30$ = diza-sita e triga).

The weekend is véta-soma (end-week). Formal texts may add a nature byname

where culturally fixed (séra-soma Sunday, sun-day).

14.4 Months

Regular numeral-based names (mava = month):

mava-ona Jan, mava-tuo Feb, mava-tro Mar, mava-fóra Apr, mava-kvín May,

mava-sixa Jun, mava-sita Jul, mava-oita Aug, mava-noa Sep, mava-diza Oct,

mava-unésa Nov, mava-dósa Dec.

14.5 Seasons

spira spring, súmmer summer (attested summér), auta autumn, winta

winter. A season is a normal noun: na spira in spring.

14.6 Writing the date

Three formats are standard; the register chooses:

RegisterFormatExample
StandardD mava-suso13 mava-tro 2026 13 March 2026
FormalD de M de SUdíza-trósa de mava-tro de díza-tuo
Technical (ISO)SU-MM-DD2026-03-13
  • Years are cardinal: tuo mila dizas-sita 2026; read digit-pairs in Technical with digit-forms (tuo-zer-tuo-sis).
  • A weekday precedes the date: soma-kvín, 13 mava-tro Friday, 13 March.
  • Decades: use na lé X-jaj susos (in the X-th years), e.g. `na lé vintaj susos in the twenties; centuries X sentsuso, e.g. vinta-ona sentsuso` the 21st century.
  • dúva date (calendar day), navás calendar/almanac.

To né tála pos tro divas. — It happened three days ago/later (§6 tense).

14.8 Duration vs. point

por + time = duration (for): Mi né kiva por tro vora. *I worked for three

hours. A bare time phrase is a point or frequency: Mi kiva tro vora. I work

three hours (each time).* Frequency uses the habitual aspect véta or the

frequency adverbs of §10.3.

14.9 Time in this Standard

Documents of the Standard carry the date in Standard format (§14.6); the virtual

reference date is 2026. Time zones are Technical-term territory

(zóna-vora time zone, UTV UTC) and are not normative in v1.x.
