# §11 Prepositions — Beluma Language Standard v1.0

Status: **Normative.**

## 11.1 The prepositional phrase

A preposition takes a noun phrase and forms a PP that follows the verb (or the
noun it modifies): `na tava` *at the market*, `de nésa` *from home*,
`ko krosa` *with a friend*. The preposition is **invariant**; its object has no
case marking.

## 11.2 Core prepositions

| Preposition | Core meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `na` | at, in, on (location) |
| `a` | to, toward (direction) |
| `de` | of, from (origin, possession source) |
| `ko` | with (together with) |
| `sen` | without |
| `li` | by (agent of a passive, §6.5) |
| `ze` | of, among (partitive; superlative frame) |
| `ban` | than (comparison) |
| `kom` | as, like (equality) |
| `por` | for, in order to (purpose, beneficiary) |
| `tra` | through |
| `pre` | past, beyond, by (beside) |
| `sirka` | around |
| `inter` | between, among |
| `subo`/`na subo` | under |
| `supo`/`na supo` | over, above |
| `ante` | before (time/order) |
| `pos` | after |
| `dum` | during |
| `kontra` | against |
| `pro` | because of, on account of |
| `da-` | away from (prefix-like, `dé-miva`) |

## 11.3 Location vs. direction

Three-way distinction:

- **place** `na` — `Mi esto na tiva.` *I am in the room.*
- **goal** `a` — `Mi gai a tiva.` *I go to the room.*
- **source** `de` — `Mi gai de tiva.` *I come from the room.*

Motion verbs select `a`/`de`; stative verbs and `esto` select `na`.

## 11.4 Preposition + pronoun

Pronouns take their **subject** form after a preposition in Standard
(`ko mi` *with me*, `na tu` *at you*); the object forms are used only in Informal
(`ko mu`, `na tu`). Possessive `-la` supplies the "of" relation without a noun:
`mi-la` *mine* (§04.4).

## 11.5 Prepositions as verb particles

`luma-dora` *turn on* and similar phrasal verbs were historically `V + pre`; in
Standard they are single registered words (§6.3). A bare preposition after a verb
is a normal PP, not a particle: `hura supo nésa` *fly above the house*.

## 11.6 Stranding and omission

Beluma **never strands** a preposition: the PP stays together (`lé misi ka mi
kosa ko` *the person I speak with* — literally "the person that I speak with",
with `ko` final but still attached to its (ellipted) object). The object may be
omitted when recoverable from context; the preposition may not.

## 11.7 Compound prepositions

Two-word and hyphenated prepositions are normal: `de-ante` *from before*,
`na-inter` *in between*, `dé-miva` *away from*. They behave as single units for
placement.
