# §16 Technology and Computing — Beluma Language Standard v1.0

Status: **Normative** for the naming patterns; **Informative** for the word list
(the living list is `lexicon/h.txt`, tag `[technical]`).

## 16.1 Principle

Technical Beluma prefers **built** words (§15.8) over loans; a loan is used when it
is already international and short (`komputa`, `programa`, `koda`, `aplika`).
Every device, process, or concept is named by head + specifier so its meaning is
inferable: `kiva-siva-nira` *phone* = work-voice-small.

## 16.5 Internet and social media

`afiŝo` post, `komento` comment, `ŝatato` like, `abonanto` follower/subscriber,
`kunhavigo` share, `memeo` meme, `tendencо` trend, `blogo`, `vlogo`,
`podkasto`, `elsendo` stream, `reto influanto` influencer, `novaĵo` news,
`falsaj novaĵoj` fake news, `privata mesaĝo` direct message, `kaŝnomo`
pseudonym, `trolo` troll.

## 16.6 Cybersecurity

`kibersekureko` cybersecurity, `hakado` hacking, `malica programo` malware,
`fajroŝirmilo` firewall, `ŝifrado`/`malŝifrado` encryption/decryption,
`ŝlosilo` key, `aŭtentigo` authentication, `permesilo` token/permission,
`vundebleko` vulnerability, `korektaĵo` patch, `anonimigo` anonymization.

## 16.7 Space exploration

`kosmoŝipo` spaceship, `kosmostasio` space station, `satelito`, `sondilo` probe,
`lanĉo` launch, `astronaŭto`, `kosmokostumo` spacesuit, `orbitо` orbit,
`lumsemojaro` light-year, `raketo` rocket, `kosmopordo` spaceport.

## 16.8 Register

All of §16 is **Technical** register (§23.1): digit-forms for numbers (§13.2),
loans respelled, abbreviations allowed (`AI`, `PDF`, `HTTP`). Terms are promoted
to Standard as they enter daily use, via a VTXT change (§15.12).
