17.1 The scientific method in Beluma

sienko science, esploro research, eksperimento experiment, mezuro

measurement, observo observation, hipotezo, teorio, pruvo proof,

rezulto, kaŭzo/efiko cause/effect. A discovery is nova-kéma (*new

knowing*); a law of nature is natur-leĝo (distinct from legal leĝo, §18).

17.5 Reading measurements

A measurement is number + unit (+ +/− + precision):

  • tuo métra e kvín — 2.5 m (two metres and five tenths).
  • triga grado — 30°.
  • diza mílisekundo? — prefer diza miliséli ten milliseconds.
  • Ranges use a… a…: diza a vinta grado 10–20°.
  • Tolerance: plus-minus diza porsenta ±10%.

17.6 Physical and life sciences

Vocabulary follows the same head+specifier logic: atomo, molekulo,

elemento, energio, materio, maso, forto, movado, rapido,

akselo, ondo, partiklo, nukleo, elektrono; ĉelo cell, gene,

genomo, proteino, enzimo, bakterio, viruso, spesio, evoluo,

ekosistemo. A species name is X-specio; a subfield is the head + -kéma.

17.7 Precision and uncertainty

State precision with presize (precisely) or sirka (about): `sirka

senta grado` about 100°. Scientific register forbids ambiguous colloquial

degree words (tro) in published data; use mésa ban / mísa ban (§9.4).

17.8 Register

Scientific writing is Technical (§23.1): Arabic numerals, digit-forms for

spoken strings, SI units, [technical] terms. A popular-science text may switch

to Standard but must keep units and numbers consistent.
