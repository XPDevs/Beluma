# §21 Example Texts — Beluma Language Standard v1.0

Status: **Informative.** These passages illustrate the registers of §23 and are
checked by `tools/check_text.py`. Glosses follow each line.

## 21.1 A fable (Standard)

> `Ia onji soma, o nira muso un né lora o saro.`
> *One day, a small mouse saw the sky.*

> `Lu un né gida de va al o tea, ke lu un né véta kima fura.`
> *It wanted to go home, so it always sought food.*

> `O goda muso un né gida ke lu s tira.`
> *The great mouse said that it was strong.*

> `Ia tela, o nira muso un né daki ke o goda muso un s kova.`
> *But in truth, the small mouse found that the great mouse was afraid.*

> `Kéma sola libera.`
> *Knowledge alone frees.*

## 21.2 A dialogue (Informal)

> — `Sala, Tuma!`
> *Hi, Tuma!*
> — `Sala, Mila! Wé tu gida fura?`
> *Hi, Mila! Will you want food?*
> — `Nja, mi un dá fura. Nakos!`
> *No, I already ate. Thanks!*
> — `Ravi, duna!`
> *Goodbye, friend!*

Fusions `dá` (*ate*) and topic/particles `nja`, `duna` are Informal-only
(§23.1). In Standard: `Wé tu gida fura?` → `Wé tu gida fura?`; `mi un né
fura-mira` *I have eaten*.

## 21.3 A scientific note (Technical)

> `Lé métra s lé baza unora de longo na lé SI.`
> *The metre is the base unit of length in SI.*

> `Senta centimétra s onji métra; mila métra s onji kilometro.`
> *A hundred centimetres is one metre; a thousand metres is one kilometre.*

> `Lé rapido de lumo s sirka trista miliona métra po séli.`
> *The speed of light is about three hundred million metres per second.*

## 21.4 A legal clause (Formal)

> `Séksio onésa. Lé dútas gida s pagi-ké li lé sivitanos na lé máva-ona.`
> *Section one. Taxes must be paid by citizens on the first of the month.*

> `Kiu un rukа lé dútas, lu s kulpa.`
> *Whoever breaks the law is guilty.*

## 21.5 A literary stanza (Literary)

> `Ia lé saro, mi un né lora o tea.`
> *Under the sky, I saw the home.*

> `Ia lé nela, mi un né siva o nela-kanto.`
> *Near the water, I heard the water-song.*

## 21.6 Guidance

- Keep one register per text (§23.4).
- Gloss every example for learners; a learner text should add a
  word-by-word interlinear line.
- Mark proper nouns (`Mila`, `Tuma`) and loans (`lumo`) so C3 (§24.2) holds.
