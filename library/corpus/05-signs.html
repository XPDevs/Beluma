05 · Signs of the Town (notice register · canon 09 §1)

Place: throughout Sova-Nésa, on doors, the square, and the market. Register:

legal-notice / formal. Plain, civic, and dictionary-locked VTXT 3.9.


LÉ VIMA-SULA:     "Véta-namo.  Véta-misi.  Gai-gavo."
KUMA-TAVA:         "Tava-marketa — sofavi dé lé nira-suso."
MARKETA:          "Nako-sula lé nésa-krosa.  Péra-gavo, lé sova-os s héna-ta."
HULOR-NESA:       "Lé velta-hulor s péra-ta: lé réva-sumés dé lé véta-fura
                  s téla-su po veta-misi."
NESA-GAI:         "Gavo-gai, lé misi-gai!  Noha dé lé nésa-krosa."

Even the shortest signs use the civic vocabulary of the town (gai-gavo farewell/welcome

mode, nako-sula great thanks, véta-namo the commons, tava-marketa fair, péra-gavo

polite request, veta-misi everyone, misi-gai guest, gavo-gai welcome, nira-suso

new year, réva-sumés festival, véta-fura harvest). They are readable unaided at B1+.
