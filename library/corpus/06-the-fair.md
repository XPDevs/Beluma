# 06 · The Fair (folk · festival · canon 09 §4)

**Time:** the Green giving way to harvest; `réva-sumés` — the celebration festival.
**Place:** Méra-Tava, `kuma-tava` square, riverbank by the Kémenta. **Register:** folk,
colloquial, literary. Dictionary-locked VTXT 3.9.

```
Sofavi dé lé réva-sumés!  Lé Véné s luma-va ra lé tava.  Lé kuma-sula dé
Sova-Nésa s vima-va su lé tava-marketa: lé nésa-krosa, lé misi-gai, lé
gavo-misi dé lé vima-sula, lé kéla-namo dé lé namo-sula.

"O réva-fura!" sa lé méla-kivor.  "Ké dus — lé véta-fura dé lé mava-nira s
gai-gavo po veta-misi!"

Lé vélta-hulor sa lé sova-os:  "Mi s péra ta lé véta-namo s véna-va-su.  Po
veta-misi — gavo-sova dé lé nira-suso, nako-sula dé lé kuma-sula."

Lé niva-kivor né níva-va lé nira-sóva su lé Kémenta.  Véta-misi né sa: "Kék,
zira.  Gai-gavo, Sova-Nésa!"
```

Beluma-first: the festival gathers the whole town — the commons, neighbours, guests, the
head of each household, and the heirloom keys. Civility travels across registers
(`sofavi`, `gavo-sova`, `gai-gavo`, `nako-sula`, `kék zira`, `po veta-misi`). The paper's
promises hold: the fair is for everyone.
