02 · Town Meeting (formal register · canon 09 §1–2)

Speakers: the velta-hulor (mayor / state-leader), the sova-os (the assembly),

a krosa-sula writing for the paper. Setting: the court-house row at the square,

fair-week sofavi. Register: formal velta-sova.


"Sova-os, sofavi dé lé nira-suso,"  sa lé velta-hulor.  "Lé véta-namo s véna-va-su;
lé tava-marketa s gai-gavo su lé véra-nira.  Véta-sova: lé téla s réva-va su lé
marketa, po o veta-misi."

O misi dé lé sova-os sa:  "Tu s lé velta-hulor.  Lé véta-namo s péra-ta ké"
lé velta-hulor ta lé sova-os."
"Hé, kéma-va," sa lé velta-hulor.  "Téla dé lé velta-nésa s tura-va su lé
ta-lé diva.  Véta-misi s vima-va ra lé nésa-gai."

## Notes (register)

- The mayor opens with the **time-greeting + the counted year** (`sofavi dé lé
  suso-nira` = the good morning of the new year) — polite and dated at once (canon 09 §2).
- `lé véta-namo` = the commons; `tava-marketa` = the fair; `veta-misi` = everyone.
- The paper's reporter `krosa-sula` (the town's friend) types each line `po o veta-misi`
  — for every reader — and keeps the mayor's formal speech in the formal register,
  never mixing in the colloquial (`sova-níra`).
