Sova-Néla · Issue 1 — Front Page

Register: formal (canon 07 §1), legal-scientific for the court block.

Names: Sova-Nésa (the town), Véné (the river), Méra-Tava (the market ground),

Rina (the child from episode-01), never italicized in Beluma prose.


SOVA-NÉLA                        sofavi, 12 séli 1247 suso
        sova-nela dé lé kuma-sula — po veta-misi
=============================================================

1. SÉRA SU LÉ TAVA: VÉNÉ GÉVA LÉ VAVI                    [p.1 local]

   Su lé vavi séra, lé Véné s luma-va ra lé tava-sula; lé kéla
   su lé liso né s dora li lé luma.
   At dawn the river lights up along the great shore; the key on
   the stone was opened by the light.

2. LÉ KÉLA UN RÓMA LÉ VAVI                               [p.1 news]

   Rina, o lini dé Sova-Nésa, né lósa lé kéla su lé liso ra lé
   Véné.  Siva né kéma-va ta: lé kéla s tóva-va lé tura dé o
   nésa-nira — un lé vavi.  Siva né téla ta po lé velta-tésor;
   lé velta-tésor né sa: "Ké, dus.  Ta-lé kéla s dika-ké o kéla
   dé véta namo."
   Rina, a child of Sova-Nésa, took the key from the stone by the
   river.  She understood that the key holds the door of a small
   house — not the morning.  She told the state writer; he said:
   "Yes, indeed.  That key is a key of every family."

3. LÉ VELTA: MISES-JURA UN VULNO-KÉ                      [p.2 state]

   Lé velta s sa-va ta: mises-jura un s vulno-ké li lé velta.
   Lé sulo-néna s dika-ké li lé sova-dika dé lé velta-nésa.
   The state says: human rights are not to be destroyed by the
   state.  The law is the judgment of the court of the state-house.

4. MÉTI (weather)                                        [p.4]

   Lé séra s luma-va ra lé Véné.  Vira wé gai su lé tava po lé vo.
   The sun is shining on the river.  Wind will come on the shore
   toward evening.

5. TORA-SOLA: SOVA-NÉSA NÉ ZIVA SU LÉ MÉRA-TAVA          [p.4 sport]

   Lé tora-sola dé Sova-Nésa né ziva su lé Méra-Tava, zéra
   un-ziva-sula po lé velta-nésa.
   Sova-Nésa sport won at Méra-Tava — no defeats for the state-house.

6. HURA-NIRA (HOLD-COLUMN)                               [p.4]

   "Lé Véné géva lé vavi; lé kéla s tóva-va lé tura."  — Míra
   "The river gives the morning; the key holds the door."
