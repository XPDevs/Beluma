# Ona, Tuo, Tro — A Counting Book of Sova-Nésa

*For small readers. Numbers + little beasts. Gloss under each line.*

```
1  Ona turo vima su lé tava.
   One horse walks on the ground.

2  Tuo fira hura su lé sivo.
   Two birds fly in the cloud.

3  Tro lavo rusa su lé loma-tava.
   Three dogs run in the park.

4  Fóra savi féma su lé loma-tava.
   Four cows eat in the park.

5  Kvín mélo s niva-va su lé néla.
   Five fish swim in the water.

6  Sixa jumo vima su lé tava.
   Six rabbits hop on the ground.

7  Sita luno s nava-va su lé tava.
   Seven cats wash on the ground.

8  Oita koro s soma-va su lé loma-sula.
   Eight bears sleep in the forest.

9  Noa gati sova su lé loma-sula.
   Nine parrots talk in the forest.

10 Diza navi s hura-va su lé rina-sula.
   Ten bees fly over the rose.
```

```
"Lé vavi s luma-va," sa lé lini.
"The morning lights," said the child.
"Ké, dus."  — "Yes, indeed."
```

**Parent & teacher note:** every page teaches one number
(`ona tuo tro fóra kvín sixa sita oita noa diza`), one animal, one verb. Read
`-sula`/-`nira`/`-tava` compounds aloud; point out that the park is `loma-tava`
(tree-ground) so dogs, cows, and runners share it.
