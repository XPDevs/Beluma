# Varta-Sula: Sova-Nésa (Encyclopedia — stub)

**Register:** formal encyclopedic (canon 07 §1).

## Clean entry

```
SOVA-NÉSA — o velta-nira su lé Véné, ra lé tava-sula dé lé méra.

Lé namo sa ta: "lé nésa dé lé sova" (the house of speech).  Su lé
1240-suso, o kuma-sula s títa-va su ta-lé nésa-nira; lé sova dé lé
kuma-sula s dika-ké kausa lé velta-nésa s jésa-va ra lé Kémenta,
ta-lé sulo-nésa s tóva-va lé kéla dé véta namo.

Lé vavi s luma-va su lé Véné: su lé vavi séra, lé sova-dika s
dora-va ra lé tava-sula, lé tora-sola dé lé kuma-sula s ziva-va su
lé Méra-Tava, lé sova-néla dé lé tésor s gai-va po lé kuma-sula.
```

## Facts (expanded across the series)

- **Town:** Sova-Nésa — population near two thousand (`tuo mila misés`), one paper,
  one court, one bridge, one market, one myth (the morning-key).
- **Geography:** on the Véné at the sea shore; the Kémenta bridge is the oldest
  stone; Mehra-Tava (sea-ground) is the market by the estuary.
- **Culture:** the morning-key (`episode-01-le-kela.md`); each week the paper's small
  column reads the town's letters; on `vo` (evening) the town gathers at the Kémenta
  to hear verse in the formal register.
- **Lore:** "Lé Véné géva lé vavi" — *the river gives the morning.*
  The townspeople say the key holds a door, not the dawn.
