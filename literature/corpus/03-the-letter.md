# 03 · The Letter (formal letter · canon 09 §2, §5)

**Correspondent:** Sira, writing to the paper's letters column. **To:** `Sova-Néla`,
letters column. **Register:** formal letter (`velta-sova`), civility (`kíra-sova`),
dictionary-locked VTXT 3.9.

```
Sofavi, lé velta-sova dé Sova-Néla!

Mi s péra dé lé ména-kósa: po lé kuma-sula, lé véta-namo s téla-su, lé
tava-marketa s gai-gavo su lé véra-nira.  Véta-misi né vima-va su lé
kuma-tava, né sa ta lé réva-fura s néla-sula su lé susa.  Nako-sula dé lé
nésa-krosa dé véta-namo.  Kék, mi sa ta lé vélta dé lé réva-sumés s
tura-va lé téra dé lé sova-os po veta-misi.

Ra lé vima dé lé Véné, dé lé véta-fura dé lé mava-nira,
— Sira.
```

Gloss: The letter writer praises the commons and the fair, thanks the neighbours of the
town, and calls for the council to keep the festival's ground open for everyone. Uses
the polite register: civility terms (`veta-misi`, `nésa-krosa`, `nako-sula`), the masthead
promise `po veta-misi` (for everyone), and the season words of the folk calendar
(`réva-sumés`, `véta-fura`, `sofavi`).
