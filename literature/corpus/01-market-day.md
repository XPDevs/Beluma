# 01 · Market Day (colloquial register · canon 09 §1)

**Speakers:** Nako the baker, Siva the gardener, a distant cousin visiting on market
day. **Setting:** Méra-Tava, fair morning, `tava-marketa`. **Register:** colloquial
square speech.

```
1  "Gavo-sula dé lé Méra-Tava!"  sa Nako lé nésa-krosa.
   "Civility of great Méra-Tava!" said the baker to the house-friend.

2  "Sofavi, misi-gavo!"  sa Siva.  "O réva-fura su lé marketa — lé séra s luma-va."
   "Good morning, dear one!" said Siva.  "A joy-feast at the market — she keeps the sun hot."

3  "Ké, dus."  sa Nako.  "Mis véta-misi po lé fura-sula dé lé tava-marketa."
   "Yes, indeed," said the baker.  "We all share the great food of the market day."

4  "O nésa-gai!"  sa lé misi-gai.  "Nako-sula.  Zira, lé véta-sova sa: ·véta-fura, ·véta-namo.""
   "A visit!" said the guest.  "Thanks.  Listen, the news says: harvest, commons."
```

Gloss: ordinary town-small-talk: greetings (`sofavi`), the feast of the fair, the folk
rule that the market's food is `véta-fura` (everyone's harvest) and the square belongs
to `véta-namo` (the commons) — canon 09 §3–4. The paper's folk dates appear unchanged:
`sofavi, 3 séli 1247 suso` — the third day of year 1247.
