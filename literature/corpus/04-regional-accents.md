# 04 · Regional Accents (home speech vs. official · canon 09 §5)

**Speakers:** two households, and the paper's reader. **Setting:** Sova-Nésa and the
coast, comparing `nésa-sova` (home speech) with `velta-sova` (official speech) and noting
`sova-níra` (accent). **Register:** colloquial + formal, side by side.

```
[Official, veltasova]        "Sofavi.  Mi s péra lé velta dé lé tava-marketa."
[Home, nesasova]             "Sofavi!  Tu s vima-va su lé kuma-tava — lé réva-sumés!"

[Official]                   "Lé véta-namo s véna-va-su, po veta-misi."
[Home]                       "Véta-namo!  Nako-sula po lé nésa-krosa dé lé mava-sula."

[Paper, po veta-misi]        "Véta-sova dé lé nira-suso sa: lé réva-fura dé lé
tava-marketa s lé fura dé veta-misi."
```

The same wish is spoken differently by accent (`sova-níra`): home speech is warm and
compressed; official speech is deliberate and public. Both are dictionary-locked at
VTXT 3.9.
