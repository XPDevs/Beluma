Lesson 31 · Reading gradio: from glossary to fluency

Objective: work a text the way you'll keep working it.

  1. Skim for familiar roots (compounds decode themselves: tava-sula, sova-dika).
  2. Note every word you don't know; check lexicon/h.txt and the translator.
  3. Re-read aloud. Pause at idioms — gloss them, don't translate them.

Task: read the Issue-1 front page library/sova-nela/issue-01. Mark 5 new words and the idiom vira-soma.

Lesson 32 · Register: the same thought, three ways

Objective: shift between colloquial and formal (canon 07).

  • Colloquial clips and fusions: wégai (will go), mila (my), koro? really?
  • Formal keeps particles full and uses full tense markers: mi wé gai, mi-la.
  • Literary adds -vá progressive flavour and metaphor.

Drill D2 (shift to formal):

  • mi wégai po nésa (I'm gonna go home) → mi wé gai po nésa
  • mila lavo (my dog) → mi-la lavo

Lesson 33 · The story: Episode 1 "Lé Kéla"

Objective: retell the launch episode in your own words.

  • Read library/story/episode-01-le-kela.
  • Characters: Rina, Míra. Setting: Kémenta (the bridge of knowing), the river that "gives the morning".
  • Notice the interlinear format: root-by-root glosses are your training wheels.

Drill D5 (retell): in 4 Beluma sentences, say who the key belongs to and what the river gives. Key line (episode 01, sentence 10): Lé Véné géva lé vavi. The river gives the morning.

Lesson 34 · Poetry: meter and the -vá flavour

Objective: write a tiny nira-sóva (5·7·5).

  • Read ../literature/poetry/README.md and the two poems.
  • -vá is a literary progressive variant; nira-sóva = the little-speech of haiku lines.

Drill D5: write a 5·7·5 about water, snow or the star. Model (from the dossier): the autumn poem "Supa-Tava". Your line counts, not correctness, are the drill.

Lesson 35 · The counting book economy of words

Objective: teach vocabulary through restriction (children's books).

  • Read library/childrens/ona-tuo-tro.
  • Notice it teaches numbers + animals + a story in twenty lines.
  • Animal word kit (canon, line ~338 of h.txt): lavo dog, luno cat, zema goat, tóro bull, víko deer, koli squirrel, jeko duck, pula chicken, fira bird.

Drill D3: 6 cats → sixa lunos · three goats → tro zemas · one bird → ona fira.

Lesson 36 · Encyclopedia conventions

Objective: write neutral, encyclopedic prose.

  • Read library/encyclopedia/sova-nesa.
  • Encyclopedia style = sóva-sula formal register: no pronouns where avoidable, no fused speech, definitions first.

Drill D5: write a 3-line entry for the Véné river. Model lines: Lé Véné s néla. Lé Véné géva lé vavi. — The river is water. The river gives the morning.

Lesson 37 · The dialogue bank

Objective: study real exchanges (market, family, phone, doctor, formal).

  • Open library/dialogues. Role-play each twice, once per register.

Drill D1: perform the market dialogue from memory; then redo it formally.

Lesson 38 · Opinions for the paper (Tora-sola)

Objective: write a short letter to the editor.

  • The opinion section is Tora-sola (opinions column). Formal opening: Sala, lé sova-néla, (Greetings, the newspaper,).
  • Structure: state fact (ta-clause) → judgement (s luk-sula) → call to action (imperative).

Drill D5: write a 3-sentence letter about preserving the Kémenta bridge. Model: Sala, lé Sova-Néla. Mi kéma ta lé Kémenta s kéla. Da lé vavi s gavo, lé tava s réva. — Greetings, the paper. I know the Kémenta is a key. If the morning is good, the town is happy.

Lesson 39 · The exam workshop

Objective: face the exam format calmly.

  • Work the A1 sample paper in class, then the A2 and B1 papers under time.
  • Sections: reading (facts), gaps (grammar), translation (both ways), listening (script), writing (register shift), speaking (role card).

Drill: mark yourself with the official key; note which lessons to redo.

Lesson 40 · The end-of-course project

Objective: prove you can use the whole system.

  • Read the front page, retell episode 01, and compose a 5-sentence formal letter to Tora-sola.
  • Revisit the lesson-07 exchange and perform it in the formal register.
  • Stand before the adult exam board (or the mirror) and pass your oral.
