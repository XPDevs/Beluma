Lía Beluma — A1 Sample Paper ("Lé Sova-Kéma nas A O NA")

sofavi, 12 séli 1247 suso · 45 minutes · pass ≥ 60% · answer key at the end

Section 1 · Reading — Lé lóva (25%)

Read the three lines of the Issue-1 front page extract, then answer.

Lé tava s gavo. Lé vavi s séra-éla. Lé gino gava-géva furas na nésa-gava.

1.1 Write the food the girl sells, in Beluma.

1.2 What does <gino> mean in English?

1.3 Is the morning fair? Give the sentence from the extract.

Section 2 · Grammar — Lé sova-kéma (25%)

2.1 Make plural: lavo → ____ · fira → ____ · nésa → ____

2.2 Make possessive: mi + lavo → ____ (my dog)

2.3 Make future: Siva gai. → ____

2.4 Make negative: Mi kéma. → ____

2.5 Make a question: Tu gai po nésa. → ____

Section 3 · Translation — Lé dis-sova (20%)

(en→be) 3.1 I am happy. 3.2 The bird is good. 3.3 She will sing.

(be→en) 3.4 Mi né féma lé fura. 3.5 Kvo tu kima gai vél mi?

Section 4 · Listening — Lé héna-sova (15%)

Examiner reads twice: "Mi s Rina. Mi s su lé Kémenta. Mi s gino su lé liso."

4.1 Who is speaking? 4.2 Where is she? 4.3 What is the last word you hear?

Section 5 · Writing — Lé vésa-sova (10%)

Write two Beluma sentences introducing yourself (name, where you live, one fact about

the morning). Use the formal register (no wégai, no mila).

Section 6 · Speaking — Lé sova-tiva (5%)

Role card drawn: "An old friend you meet at the Méra-Tava market." Say hello, ask how he

is, ask where he is going, and offer to buy fruit together. Practice both roles as examiner

and candidate.

Answer key (examiner's copy)

Section 1: 1.1 fura (fruit) — accept furas. 1.2 girl. 1.3 Yes — Lé vavi s séra-éla.

Section 2: 2.1 lavos, firas, nésas. 2.2 mi-la lavo. 2.3 Siva wé gai.

2.4 Mi un kéma. 2.5 Kvo tu gai po nésa?

Section 3: 3.1 Mi s réva. 3.2 Lé fira s gavo. 3.3 Siva wé níva.

3.4 I ate the fruit. 3.5 Can you come with me?

Section 4: 4.1 Rina. 4.2 At/by the Kémenta bridge (accept "on the stone bridge").

4.3 liso — the last word is the stone.

Gloss of the script: "I am Rina. I am at the Kémenta. I am a girl on the stone."

Section 5 (model): Mi s Rina. Mi s su lé Kémenta. Lé vavi s gavo. — formal, no fusions.

Section 6 (model opening): Sala, krosa! Havi tu s? Kwaras tu gai? Kvo tú níma gava-pira furas vél mi?

Examiner's notes

  • In 3.5 accept any of come/go (gai) clauses with vél mi ("with me").
  • In 4.1 accept "the girl Rina"; the point is the name, not the pronoun.
  • In Section 6, credit any two of the four required moves; the formal register is not required at A1 (assess at A2+).
