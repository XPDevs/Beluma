Lía Beluma — The Certification Program (Phase 5)

The Lía Beluma Exam is the official proficiency test of the Sova-Nésa world: six

papers from A1 to C2 on the Council scale, run twice a year at the Kémenta assembly

hall. The format below is the reference for teachers, examiners, and the computer-based

session.

Passing marks: ≥ 60% overall; ≥ 50% in each section. Paper level is printed in Beluma

A O NA ("A one") etc.; the exam session opens with the greeting of the day

(sofavi = good morning).

Total ~45 minutes at A1, growing to 3 hours plus oral at C2.

3. Marking rules

  • Translation section: 1 point per item, half-point for correct grammar but a wrong word.
  • Writing: register fidelity counts (formal paper, not colloquial fusion, at B1+).
  • The answer key always gives (a) expected form, (b) acceptance notes for elegant alternatives coined within canon 06.

4. Delivering the exam session

  • Reading & grammar: printed papers from the papers/ folder (A1 sample in this file).
  • Listening: examiner reads the script twice, normal speed, then once glossed.
  • Speaking: role cards drawn at random; one minute preparation, three minutes perform.
  • Computer session: the same papers work with any self-hosted render of the translator; candidates check their own translations for homework.

Note on dates: papers use the folk calendar of the Sova-Néla: sofavi, 12 séli 1247 suso

(the header of the newspaper issue). In the folk count a séli is a day-step of the sun

and suso the year; the civil (modern) count replaces the count with mavas (months:

12 mavas). The exam board prints both so candidates are never tested on calendar math.
